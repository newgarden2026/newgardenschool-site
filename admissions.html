Step by Step

How to Apply

Applying to New Garden School is a straightforward process. Our team is always available to answer questions and guide your family through each step.

1
Request an Application Form
Contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email — or visit us in person during office hours. Application forms are available on request at no charge.
2
Complete & Submit Your Application
Fill in the form carefully and return it with all required supporting documents. A complete application ensures the fastest possible processing time.
3
Child Interview & Placement Assessment
Every child is unique. The interview allows our educators to assess your child's current level, social skills and any specific needs — so we can provide the best possible placement and support.
4
Receive Our Decision
Once reviewed, families are notified within 5 business days. The full process typically takes 2–3 weeks from submission. Apply early to avoid disappointment.
5
Enrol & Secure Your Child's Place
If accepted, you have 2 weeks to submit enrolment paperwork and confirm your child's spot. Places are not held beyond this window.

How to Pay

Payment Options

We offer several convenient payment methods to accommodate all families. Please always use your child's full name and surname as the payment reference.

1
EFT (Electronic Transfer)
Pay via EFT directly into our account. Contact the finance office to request our banking details. Use your child's full name and surname as the payment reference.
2
Swipe or Tap at the School
Card payments are accepted at the school office. Visit us during finance office hours to make a payment in person.
3
Cash at the School Office
Cash payments are accepted at the school office. Always request an official receipt for any cash payment made.

Safety & Security at NGS

At New Garden School, the safety of every student is our highest priority:

  • 24-hour CCTV surveillance across the entire campus
  • Access-controlled gates — only authorised personnel may enter
  • First-Aid station staffed with trained first responders
  • Regular fire-safety drills and extinguishers throughout
  • Unannounced spot-checks for alcohol and drugs
  • DBE–SAPS Collaborative Partnership Protocol
  • Trained staff and student prefects on duty daily
