Our Approach

A Well-Rounded Education

Our school provides a comprehensive education programme for Grades R to 9, with a focus on nurturing and supporting every student throughout their school life. Our experienced and dedicated teachers work hard to create a safe and stimulating environment for learning.

We follow the official CAPS curriculum and have expanded our language programme to include IsiXhosa alongside English and Afrikaans. We are proud to be a DoE Pilot School for the developing Grade R to 9 Coding & Robotics curriculum.

Computer Laboratory
Dedicated computer lab for coding, computer literacy and robotics across all phases.
Art Room
A dedicated creative space where students explore visual arts and creative expression.
Extensive Library
A well-stocked library catering to all age groups and fostering a love of reading.

Safety & Security

A Safe Environment for Learning

At New Garden School, safety and security is our top priority. Our campus features state-of-the-art security measures and a trained, vigilant team ensuring every student is protected at all times.

01
24h Security System
State-of-the-art 24-hour surveillance system, access-controlled gates and secure parking across the campus at all times.
02
Trained Teachers & Staff
All staff are specially trained to identify and respond to dangerous situations. Regular fire-safety drills ensure full preparedness.
03
Building Design & Fire Systems
Fire extinguishers are readily available throughout the premises. Regular fire safety training is conducted for all staff and students.
04
Responsible & Vigilant Students
Trained student prefects assist in emergency situations. We take health and safety seriously at every level of the school.
Part of the DBE–SAPS Collaborative Partnership Protocol
New Garden School is registered with the Collaborative Partnership Protocol between the Department of Basic Education and the South African Police Service. We make regular use of a security company and the Police to maintain a zero-risk environment.

Safety & Security at NGS

At New Garden School, the safety of every student is our highest priority. Our comprehensive security system includes:

  • 24-hour CCTV surveillance across the entire campus
  • Access-controlled gates — only authorised personnel may enter
  • First-Aid station staffed with trained first responders
  • Regular fire-safety drills and extinguishers throughout
  • Unannounced spot-checks for alcohol and drugs
  • Part of the DBE–SAPS Collaborative Partnership Protocol
  • Trained staff and student prefects on duty daily
