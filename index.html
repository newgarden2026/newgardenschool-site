Our Story

A School Built on Purpose & Care

Founded by Mrs. Shelly Cook in 1995, New Garden School began as a small preschool in a converted house in Stutterheim. Today, governed by the New Garden Educational Development Trust, we serve nearly 200 students from Grade R to 9.

Our principal Mr. Myburgh leads with a clear philosophy: education must develop the whole child — academically, morally and socially.

  • Independent NPO & PBO — registered with the Dept. of Education since 2006
  • Official CAPS Curriculum — nationally recognised and accredited
  • Coding & Robotics Pilot School — one of the first in the Eastern Cape
  • Trilingual — English, Afrikaans and IsiXhosa

From Grade R to Grade 9, our structured phases provide the right environment, teachers and resources at every stage of your child's development.

Safety & Security

Your Child's Safety Is Our Priority

Comprehensive, layered security measures are in place at all times. Parents can trust New Garden School is a safe, well-supervised environment every day.

01
24-Hour Surveillance
State-of-the-art CCTV covering all areas of campus, with access-controlled gates and secure parking.
02
Trained Staff & Prefects
All staff trained to identify and respond to dangerous situations. Student prefects assist with daily supervision.
03
Fire Safety
Fire extinguishers throughout the premises, regular evacuation drills and emergency-compliant building design.
04
First-Aid Ready
Fully stocked First-Aid station with trained first-response personnel available at all times.
05
Access Controlled
Only authorised persons enter campus. Unannounced spot-checks for alcohol and drugs. Regular security patrols.
06
DBE–SAPS Partnership
Part of the Collaborative Partnership Protocol between the Department of Basic Education and the SAPS.

Safety & Security at NGS

At New Garden School, the safety of every student is our highest priority. Our comprehensive security system includes:

  • 24-hour CCTV surveillance across the entire campus
  • Access-controlled gates — only authorised personnel may enter
  • First-Aid station staffed with trained first responders
  • Regular fire-safety drills and extinguishers throughout
  • Unannounced spot-checks for alcohol and drugs
  • Part of the DBE–SAPS Collaborative Partnership Protocol
  • Trained staff and student prefects on duty daily
