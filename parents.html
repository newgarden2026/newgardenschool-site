Getting Here Safely

Arriving at School

We understand that drop-offs and pick-ups can be hectic, which is why we have created a dedicated safe zone to ensure that students arrive and depart safely every single day.

Dedicated Safe Zone
A clearly marked drop-off and collection area designed to minimise congestion and ensure every child arrives safely.
Taxi Collaboration
We have worked with local taxi-drivers to ensure a safe, quick and timely drop-off and pick-up for all students using public transport.
Teacher & Prefect Supervision
Vigilant staff and student prefects keep a close eye on traffic and learners at all times during arrival and departure.
Access-Controlled Gates
Only authorised adults may enter the school premises. Our gates ensure your child is always in a secure environment.
School starts at 07:20
Please ensure your child arrives on time. Late arrivals disrupt the class and affect learning. Gates open from 07:00.

What to Wear

School Uniform

Get your child ready for school in style with our well-designed, high-quality School Uniform — available for purchase directly at the school.

  • Red and black colours — our school's badge and branding incorporated throughout the uniform.
  • Available at school — purchase the official uniform directly from us to guarantee the correct items.
  • Fitting & dressing room — a dedicated space available to ensure a perfect fit before you buy.
  • High-quality materials — durable, comfortable and smart for the full school day.
Friday Civvie Day
Every Friday is Civvie Day — students dress in their own clothes and enjoy a fun end to the week, with special treats as part of the celebrations!

Staying Connected

Holidays & Absences

We know that life can get busy and sometimes things come up — whether a planned holiday or an unexpected absence. Don't worry; we have clear procedures to ensure your child doesn't fall behind.

Daily Attendance Tracking
We keep a careful record of absentees every single day so no child falls through the cracks.
Missed Lessons Sent to You
We have procedures in place to ensure that missed work and lessons are sent directly to parents when a child is absent.
WhatsApp Class Groups
Our parent WhatsApp groups keep you in the loop with daily school news, notices and updates.
Planned absence?
Please notify the office in writing or via email before your child's absence. This allows us to prepare missed work in advance.
Always in the Loop
Affordable & Tasty
Nutrition & Well-Being

Daily Menu &
Tuck Shop

At New Garden School, we understand the importance of healthy, affordable food for our students. A well-fed child is a focused and happy learner.

  • Bring your own lunchbox — we encourage students to bring nutritious food and a drink from home each day.
  • Tuck shop on campus — affordable snacks and drinks available for students who need them.
  • Friday special treats — special snacks are part of the Civvie Day Friday celebrations every week!
  • Well-being focus — we take care of the whole child, including every aspect of their health and nutrition.
We strive for well-being
We aim to create a positive learning environment that takes care of every aspect of your child's well-being — inside and outside the classroom.

Safety & Security at NGS

At New Garden School, the safety of every student is our highest priority. Our comprehensive security system includes:

  • 24-hour CCTV surveillance across the entire campus
  • Access-controlled gates — only authorised personnel may enter
  • First-Aid station staffed with trained first responders
  • Regular fire-safety drills and extinguishers throughout
  • Unannounced spot-checks for alcohol and drugs
  • Part of the DBE–SAPS Collaborative Partnership Protocol
  • Trained staff and student prefects on duty daily
