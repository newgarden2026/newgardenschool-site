A Little History

How New Garden Began

New Garden School was started by Mrs. Shelly Cook in 1995. She decided that having a preschool close to the Stutterheim High School would be beneficial for both parents and children — and altered the house on the property to make it more suitable as a pre-school.

Over the years, the school developed and grew into an institution offering quality education from Grade R to 9.

1995
Mrs. Shelly Cook opens New Garden as a preschool in a converted house near Stutterheim High School.
2006
Registered with the Department of Education as an independent institution. Operating as an NPO and PBO.
2011
Ownership transferred to the newly established New Garden Educational and Developmental Trust (NGEDT).
Today
Nearly 200 students, 8 new classrooms added, 15 qualified teachers, Grade R to 9 programme.
More of Our History

Established as an independent institution in 2006 and registered with the Department of Education, our school operates as a non-profit organization (NPO) and a public benefit organization (PBO).

Located in the scenic Eastern Cape, our school began with a modest number of students and a dedicated handful of teachers. In 2011, ownership was transferred to the newly established NGEDT.

Since then, our student body has grown significantly, reaching nearly 200 students. We have expanded by adding eight new classrooms and continue to enhance our existing buildings. Our team has grown from a few educators to an entire staff dedicated to high-quality education.

Our Mission

We believe that every child matters, and we strive to provide an education that reflects that belief. Our teachers are passionate about education and work tirelessly to create lessons and experiences that are engaging and effective.

Safety & Security

We Are All About Safety

At New Garden School, the safety of our students is our highest priority. We have comprehensive, layered security measures in place — so parents can trust their child is protected every day.

01
24/7 Surveillance
State-of-the-art CCTV covering all areas of campus, with access-controlled gates and secure parking at all times.
02
Trained & Vigilant Staff
All staff trained to identify and respond calmly and effectively in dangerous situations. Safety protocols reviewed regularly.
03
Fire Safety & Readiness
Fire extinguishers throughout the premises, regular evacuation drills, and emergency-compliant building design and alarms.
04
First-Aid Ready
Fully stocked First-Aid station and personnel trained in basic First-Aid on site at all times.
05
Access-Controlled
Only authorised persons enter the campus. Unannounced spot-checks for alcohol and drugs are conducted regularly.
06
DBE–SAPS Partnership
Part of the Collaborative Partnership Protocol between the Department of Basic Education and the South African Police Service.
Zero-Risk Environment
We conduct unannounced spot-checks for alcohol and drugs. We make regular use of a security company and the Police. We are part of the DBE–SAPS Collaborative Partnership Protocol. Safety comes first — always.
